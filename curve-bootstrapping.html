How it works

Prices are FedInvest's end-of-day quotes, clean and per 100 of face value, read from a daily snapshot of TreasuryDirect. Each security with a price on the chosen side becomes one node of the curve, at its maturity. Nodes are solved in order of maturity: each one sets the discount factor at which the security's remaining coupons and principal, discounted off the curve built so far, add up to its price plus accrued interest.

Discount factors are interpolated log-linearly between nodes, so the instantaneous forward rate is flat between two maturities and jumps at each node. Zero rates and forwards are continuously compounded. Par yields are the semiannual coupons at which a bond issued on the valuation date and maturing at each standard tenor prices at par off the curve, the same basis as Treasury's published par yield curve.
